About

The Short Version

Judy is a relationship and business development professional with a deep specialization in payments, FX, and cross-border money movement. She currently manages strategic enterprise, high-risk vertical client merchants across the EEA region at Rapyd.

Her background spans enterprise account management, merchant acquiring, and international FX payments across some of the biggest names in payments and fintech - Worldpay, Rapyd, Wallex, and American Express. While leading Travel & Airlines APAC at Worldpay, she handled airline clients across Hong Kong and Greater China, and is highly proficient in professional Mandarin - both in presentations and negotiations.

She's a strong communicator who builds trust quickly across internal and external stakeholders, and brings an unusual dual lens to the work - she originally trained as an architect (Master of Architecture, NUS), which shapes how she thinks about structure, client needs, and systems.

Outside of work, she's an avid builder - currently down a 3D printing rabbit hole - alongside pilates, pottery, and reading. She's also active in the SG Claude community, where she's building and exploring in the online space too.
